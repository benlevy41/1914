# <p align="center">1914</p>
## <p align="center">The Western Front</p>

### Background: 
In August, 1914 the German Empire invaded Belgium, violating Belgian neutrality and drawing France, Russia and the United Kindom into the conflict that would become the first World War.  Widely known for the brutal and mostly static trench warfare, the Western Front initially saw a rapid war of movement as the German army quickly pushed through Belgium and Luxembourg and into northern France. Only German missteps and a few heroic defensive actions on the part of the Allied forces kept the Germans from sweeping through into Paris.  As the guns of August roared, history hung in the balance and no one knew how this war would end...

### Objective:
The object of the game is complete destruction of enemy forces and capture of their respective centers of gravity: Paris for the Allies, and Koblenz* for the German Empire. To win, a player must capture and occupy all 32 cities on the map.

\* *Note that although Koblenz was a strategic HQ for the German military, the capitol of the German Empire was Berlin.  Koblenz was selected as the German COG primarily because Berlin is too far to fit on the game board!*

### Setup:
The game begins with each player assigned a random draw of 15 cities and their respective center of gravity.  Each occupied city has one unit of infantry. Players have five additional infantry units that can be placed on any city occupied by their forces.  These reserve units are placed one at a time by turns, starting with the German Empire, until all troops have been emplaced.  

### Game Play:
Each turn consists of three phases: reinforcement, attack, and reorganization.  At the beginning of the turn, each player is assigned a number of reinforcements based on the number of territories that they hold at the start of the turn.  Every five cities occupied by the player contribute one infantry unit, and no partial units are assigned.  These reinforcements must be placed in cities that are connected to the player's center of gravity by roads that are completely controlled by that player.  Isolated cities cannot be reinforced.  If a player's center of gravity is occupied by the enemy, they will not receive reinforcements for that turn.  All reinforcements must be placed before attacking.  

Players can occupy an abandoned city or attack a city occupied by the enemy that is directly connected to one of their cities by a road.  The player can choose to attack with any number of units up to the number occupying that city.  Combat is decided by rolling dice, with each attacking unit contributing a single roll of a die. The defending player is allowed to commit any number of forces to combat up to the number in the defending garrison, but must commit at least one. The highest rolling dice of both sides are compared and the lower roll loses.  If a roll is equal, the defender gets the advantage.  Each losing roll results in one unit from that player being removed from the field.  An attacking player may choose to attack as long as they have forces to commit.  If during the attacking phase the defenders are eliminated from a garrison, the attacking player can choose to move any number of troops from the attacking garrison into the defending garrison but must keep at least one unit in the attacking garrison.  If the attacker has only one unit left in the attacking garrison, the defending garrison must remain unoccupied until the next turn - cities cannot be captured or occupied outside of the attacking phase. Likewise, if an attacking player loses all units in a garrison, the garrison will remain unoccupied.

After the player is finished attacking, either by choice or because they have no more troops to commit, they begin the reorganization phase.  During reorganization, a player can move units along contiguous roadways that are fully occupied by their forces.  Isolated contiguous sections of roads can also be traversed - they do not have to be connected to the player's center of gravity.  A player may choose to abandon a city, leaving it unoccupied. Unoccupied cities cannot be traversed or occupied during the reorganization phase.  After reorganization is complete, the turn is over and the opposing player starts their turn.
